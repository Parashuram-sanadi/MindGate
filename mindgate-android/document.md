# MindGate Quiz Challenge Types

MindGate provides various mindful challenges to help users pause, breathe, and regain focus before granting extra screen time. Each challenge is designed to test different cognitive aspects and educate the user on digital wellness.

## 1. Math Challenge (Numerical Focus)
*   **Description**: Dynamically generated arithmetic problems involving addition, subtraction, or multiplication.
*   **Logic**:
    *   Addition and Subtraction use operands between 100 and 500.
    *   Multiplication uses one operand between 100 and 500, and a multiplier between 2 and 9.
*   **Objective**: Engages the brain in structured logical thinking to disrupt mindless scrolling patterns.

## 2. Wellness Trivia (Habit Awareness)
*   **Description**: Multiple-choice questions specifically curated around digital health, sleep hygiene, and ergonomics.
*   **Sample Topics**:
    *   Recommended sleep duration for students.
    *   The 20-20-20 rule for eye strain.
    *   Hormonal impact of blue light (Melatonin suppression).
    *   Terminology awareness (e.g., FOMO).
*   **Objective**: To reinforce digital wellness knowledge and encourage healthier habits.

---

### Challenge Configuration
*   **Retry Limit**: Users have 3 attempts to pass a challenge.
*   **Timer**: 
    *   Math: 60 seconds
    *   Trivia: 90 seconds
*   **Outcome**: Successful completion grants a calibrated extension (typically 15 minutes) and logs the activity for parent visibility.
